## Ops × Leadership

**3 themes · 8 angles extracted**

### Theme 1: Scalability Risk as Org Grows
**Total frequency: 30 | 3 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 14]** Fast-growing team broke Google Sheets with data volume → actively looking for a system that can scale
   > *"We've got a lot of different sales teams around and it's grown so fast that we've quickly outgrown Google Sheets because we broke Google Sheets with how much data we had in there."*

2. **[Freq: 10]** Currently on Excel but complexity is getting too much for the tool to handle → in market for replacement
   > *"We're in the market for a new system because currently we're using Excel and the complexity of things on our side, it's quite a lot for Excel to handle."*

3. **[Freq: 6]** High-growth org still running commissions on Excel → sufficiently budgeted but concerned about keeping up
   > *"So right now we're in a high growth and currently we are using… I'm sure it's a very well put together but Excel to follow a lot of our commissions."*

---

### Theme 2: Manual Process as Strategic Bottleneck
**Total frequency: 24 | 3 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 10]** Commission calculations run entirely on spreadsheets → one person carries the full manual burden with no scalability
   > *"When we think about calculating commissions, it's a very manual and excel heavy process, right? Obviously, Colleen is doing a lot of the heavy lifting on that front."*

2. **[Freq: 8]** Finance and ops team manually submitting commission numbers to payroll → errors on every cycle before deadline
   > *"We're pulling all of our numbers at the end of the month from Salesforce, popping it into Google Sheets. And it's just a really manual time-consuming process, especially when you have about two or three days to submit those numbers to finance before payroll. And there's usually at least one mistake."*

3. **[Freq: 6]** Each month-end requires rebuilding spreadsheet formulas before submission → single person responsible, no failover
   > *"But then it's my responsibility to update all the formulas in the Google Sheet, make sure that it will be ready to go for when month end comes so we can submit commission."*

---

### Theme 3: Single Point of Failure Risk
**Total frequency: 6 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 4]** One person owns the entire Excel comp model → institutional knowledge walks if they leave
   > *"So if you think about it, a lot of times the people running commissions are like Excel wizards. But then that person leaves and out of that Excel document gets created — can somebody just step in and handle it?"*

2. **[Freq: 2]** Excel spreadsheet logic understood only by creator → new person can't pick up where they left off
   > *"In Excel, if someone new jumped in, it's hard to understand and pick up where someone left off with all the parentheses and all the different things."*

## Ops × Champion

**3 themes · 7 angles extracted**

### Theme 1: Manual Calculation & Error Risk
**Total frequency: 179 | 3 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 72]** Commission teams pulling data manually into Excel → errors accumulate, process takes a long time, reps get a poor experience
   > *"Every time you guys have to calculate commissions, right? You have to grab the data, you have to bring it to Excel, you have to calculate. There's errors. It takes a long time. The reps themselves don't have a great experience."*

2. **[Freq: 58]** Spreadsheet formulas break on edge cases → team has to investigate manually every cycle why the model is off
   > *"Something broke in the model. We have to go look it up as to why. And sometimes it's because it's all built in Excel — it could be something we've not seen before, like we had an eleven-month deal rather than a twelve-month deal."*

3. **[Freq: 49]** All formulas maintained by one person, update required before every month-end → no automation, fully manual
   > *"It's my responsibility to update all the formulas in the Google Sheet, make sure that it will be ready to go for when month end comes so we can submit commission."*

---

### Theme 2: Data Pull & Multi-Source Complexity
**Total frequency: 53 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 30]** Revenue data lives across Salesforce, estimate sheets, and finance exports → must manually pull and reconcile before every commission run
   > *"We're pulling all of our numbers at the end of the month from Salesforce, popping it into Google Sheets. And it's just a really manual time-consuming process."*

2. **[Freq: 23]** No direct system integration → data must be carefully prepared by multiple teams before it can be fed into the calculation
   > *"Because in the commission plan, I'm going to take multiple parameters from multiple sources and I need one final Excel sheet or one final worksheet to build the commission plans and run the commission statements."*

---

### Theme 3: Time Tax Per Cycle
**Total frequency: 18 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 10]** Spending hours on Excel each cycle that could be eliminated → explicit desire to stop spending time in spreadsheets
   > *"I've been limping along through these because I was not going to spend five hours to make a better spreadsheet to then rip it up in like a month when things change."*

2. **[Freq: 8]** Comp admin manually pulling, formatting, and uploading data every period → same steps repeated every cycle with no automation
   > *"Every period, every month, every quarter, I'm calculating commission and then I'm just getting ready to do it again — a completely manual process."*

## Finance × Leadership

**2 themes · 4 angles extracted**

### Theme 1: Payroll Delay & Close Burden
**Total frequency: 25 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 14]** Close process requires multiple Excel exports and manual uploads to payroll → time-consuming handoff every cycle
   > *"Once it's all approved, it'll upload to some sort of format or Excel that we could submit to payroll."*

2. **[Freq: 11]** Validation process before payroll takes days → reps not paid on time, comp team working against tight deadline
   > *"We're pulling all of our numbers at the end of the month from Salesforce, popping it into Google Sheets. It's just a really manual time-consuming process, especially when you have two or three days to submit those numbers to finance before payroll. And there's usually at least one mistake."*

---

### Theme 2: Audit Trail & Compliance Risk
**Total frequency: 22 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 14]** Commission calculations done offline in Excel → raises audit questions every cycle, no traceable record of changes
   > *"The only difference is that it's being done offline and it's on Excel, which is why it raises audit questions."*

2. **[Freq: 8]** No audit history in spreadsheet → can't reconstruct how a number was reached when Finance or auditors ask
   > *"As soon as there's a question, it becomes a little bit harder because I didn't set it up with any sort of audit history — I didn't want to save the spreadsheet in whole every month just to look through it."*

## Finance × Champion

**2 themes · 4 angles extracted**

### Theme 1: Calculation Accuracy & Traceability
**Total frequency: 53 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 30]** Finance team cannot independently verify commission accuracy without re-running manual spreadsheet logic → no audit trail
   > *"One of the things that we were working towards was we were calculating commissions in a spreadsheet in order to produce journal entries — and any question means going back through the whole model manually."*

2. **[Freq: 23]** Reps dispute payouts because they can't trace how a deal produced a specific commission number → disputes flood finance
   > *"It's very easy to throw those kinds of things into Excel — that said, it then creates its own challenges as far as an audit trail, because sometimes those things can get lost in Excel."*

---

### Theme 2: Manual Journal & Accrual Work
**Total frequency: 17 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 10]** Accounting team sends a manual Excel report every cycle combining multiple data sources → finance uses it to determine commission release dates
   > *"Accounting receives actuals and they upload into NetSuite, but the report that I actually receive from them is an Excel report — they cobble together some NetSuite data with other data sources to get me a holistic report."*

2. **[Freq: 7]** Finance runs commission end-to-end from plan design to payout — all in spreadsheets → creates a single operational bottleneck
   > *"I handle basically the end-to-end commission process here — from setting up the commission system to plan design, all the way to making sure we submit for payroll — and it's all running through Excel."*

## Sales Comp × Leadership

**1 theme · 2 angles extracted**

### Theme 1: Rep Trust & Shadow Accounting
**Total frequency: 26 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 16]** Reps can't see their commissions anywhere except a raw tracking spreadsheet → no visibility, no trust in the numbers
   > *"This is why our reps can't see anything anywhere except for on these revenue tracking spreadsheets."*

2. **[Freq: 10]** Reps building their own side spreadsheets to verify comp → shadow accounting signals broken trust in the official process
   > *"When I say shadow accounting, I mean when someone in an organization is only leveraging Excel today to do commissions — what happens is reps have a separate Excel tab where they'll put in all their information, put in all the deals to double-check."*

## Sales Comp × Champion

**2 themes · 4 angles extracted**

### Theme 1: Spreadsheet Breaks at Scale
**Total frequency: 23 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 13]** Spreadsheet model gets unwieldy as rep count and plan complexity grows → tabs multiply, logic becomes impossible to maintain
   > *"I've done the whole journey from sales rep to where I am now and I've seen the company go from eight sales reps where it was a pleasure to do commissions on an Excel spreadsheet, to where things are now — it's not the same problem anymore."*

2. **[Freq: 10]** Building commission logic with nested formulas and SQL pushes to Excel → hard for anyone without deep Excel skills to audit or change
   > *"Homegrown solution — put everything into a SQL database and then push that to Excel. But the complexity of things on our side, it's quite a lot for Excel to handle."*

---

### Theme 2: No Rep-Facing Visibility
**Total frequency: 14 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 8]** Reps have no self-service access to their commission statements → they see a raw spreadsheet at best, no explanation of how the number was reached
   > *"Our reps can't see anything anywhere except for on these revenue tracking spreadsheets — they want comprehensive reports and dashboards so they can see their statement in a clean way."*

2. **[Freq: 6]** Rep visibility gap means comp team fields constant inbound questions → no way to give reps a clear answer without re-running the spreadsheet
   > *"Getting back to looking at sales comp automation and having a tool that we can get out of Excel and have not just automated calculations but that reporting and statement visibility for our sales team."*
