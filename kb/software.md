### Theme 1: Spreadsheet-Driven Commission Management
**Total frequency: 38 | 7 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 12]** All commission calculations live in Excel spreadsheets with no dedicated system → errors are inevitable, audits are impossible, and ops spends enormous time maintaining fragile workbooks
   > *"I know, you know, some of the users have exactly struggle with it and a lot of the stuff we're doing is still in excel and a lot of the sales reps are still doing shit and excel."*

2. **[Freq: 7]** Payout tracking managed entirely in Excel → manual tracking is error-prone and there is no single source of truth for compensation status
   > *"It's all manual right now. So we have to track in excel who we've paid, what on 50 percent is, and when the next 50 percent is doing eligible."*

3. **[Freq: 5]** Commission expense deferral calculated in Excel and fed into accounting → auditors struggle to map the trail back, creating time-consuming reconciliation work
   > *"We defer the commission expense over like a five year term or whatever? We do all of that in excel? And then from an auditor perspective, it's always mapping that back that we have a challenge."*

4. **[Freq: 5]** Territory and split decisions made outside the CRM in spreadsheets and results brought back into the system → duplicative, error-prone workflow with no audit trail
   > *"Right now, you might as well just do it in Excel and then its like bring in, the outcome and put it in ours and manage it in our system where we want to take more of that like in house."*

5. **[Freq: 4]** Making scenario calculators in Excel for reps is hard because you cannot model every plan, accelerator, and edge case → reps remain uncertain about earnings and ops spends time fielding questions
   > *"I've always had to like make calculators in excel, which is hard because you can't get every like scenario and plan and accelerator in those. It's really tricky."*

6. **[Freq: 3]** Multiple commission data sources with no unified system → commission plans are split across sources, creating reconciliation risk
   > *"One of our challenges is we, there are the commission plans are coming from two sources."*

7. **[Freq: 2]** Reports built manually in Excel and presented to leadership → no real-time visibility, reports must be rebuilt each cycle
   > *"You had created that on an excel sheet. We said like we can create up a pictorial view of it so that's interesting for the folks to look at."*

### Theme 2: Manual Ops Overhead and Time Cost
**Total frequency: 30 | 6 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 8]** Ops admin spends hours each week manually verifying commission accuracy line by line → direct time cost with no leverage and no scalability as headcount grows
   > *"It's a pain to every spend an hour and a half every week just manually going through to make sure that all of our commission's right."*

2. **[Freq: 6]** Account assignment errors require a manual end-of-month validation pass through all data lines → process is slow, repetitive, and dependent on a single person
   > *"We have a manual process that we go through to validate all the assignments after all the data is like I, in a final state. So we go through and validate all the assignments and make sure that nothing is unassigned."*

3. **[Freq: 5]** Merchant statement eligibility must be manually reviewed one by one each period → time consuming because each statement can be different and averages must be calculated
   > *"Every merchant statement can be different and you kind of have to go through. So it's a very manual process today. Like I have to check each merchant statement."*

4. **[Freq: 5]** Data uploads and quota inputs done via manual CSV or file upload each period → creates dependency on individuals, delays processing, and introduces version-control risk
   > *"We have to upload quotas in bulk. So this manual upload that we are referring to here is whether like we capture all the data that is needed to calculate commissions in Salesforce directly."*

5. **[Freq: 4]** Commission plan changes depend on IT or vendor involvement to re-program → business users cannot self-serve, making even small adjustments slow and expensive
   > *"With SPM, what we do is, you know, we would build a spreadsheet essentially send that spreadsheet to the it team and then they go in and program everything. So it's a big difference."*

6. **[Freq: 2]** Bandwidth constraints prevent migration off legacy tools even when leadership knows they are inadequate → the person who would do the work is the same person overwhelmed by the current process
   > *"I equally don't have any bandwidth in order to do that this year, it wasn't in or it wasn't scoped out to do a full replacement at this point in time."*

   > *"Living in spreadsheets, it's a dangerous game. You're it's only a matter of time before you mess up things really badly and people are, you know, you can't audit it, you can't do this and that, and there's just more room for error in general."*

3. **[Freq: 4]** Over-transparency in earnings estimates creates expectation mismatches → reps anchor to inflated pipeline-based numbers, causing distrust when final payout differs
   > *"It starts possibly mis stating expectations. And then I'm constantly having to cover those expectations state and there's a little bit of an inherent mis, trust that continues to breathe from that."*

4. **[Freq: 4]** Errors on a live plan must be corrected retroactively by ending and rebuilding the plan → no in-place fix capability, creating risk around effective dating and payment accuracy
   > *"If you took a plan live and then later on, you realized there was an error within the plan, you could just utilize the effective dating of it, right? So make a change on the new make, you know, clone the plan, fix the change on it, erase, you know, just fully delete the error plan."*
