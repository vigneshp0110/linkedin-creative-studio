## Ops × Leadership

**2 themes · 4 angles extracted**

### Theme 1: Mid-Year Comp Restructure Complexity
**Total frequency: 35 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 20]** Plans need to change mid-year as business evolves → current spreadsheet process makes mid-year changes painful and risky
   > *"These are ones that are not live today, but let's say you're going through a mid-quarter change or monthly change — the whole model needs to be touched."*

2. **[Freq: 15]** Team is actively planning to restructure comp plans but dreads the operational effort → the change itself is held back by tooling constraints
   > *"I know you mentioned it's important, but given that your team is potentially thinking about, okay, maybe we want to restructure the plans that we have today — how does that get managed?"*

---

### Theme 2: Account & Territory Reassignment at Scale
**Total frequency: 18 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 12]** Account reassignments mid-year require tracking multiple changes across reps → no clean way to manage this without rebuilding
   > *"If somebody leaves or maybe there's just account reassignments mid-year, we have to be able to account for one account being moved from a CSM to a different CSM or multiple accounts."*

2. **[Freq: 6]** Territory changes are a headache operationally → no system to handle the reassignment cleanly without touching commission logic
   > *"We were trying to manage to do like a territory change and it was an absolute headache."*

## Ops × Champion

**2 themes · 3 angles extracted**

### Theme 1: Plan Amendment Without Rebuilding History
**Total frequency: 7 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 5]** Mid-year plan change requires reconciling historical attainment against new structure → no clean way to do this without restating prior periods
   > *"Because we did have some mid-year plan changes in this fiscal year — will you need all the plan information going back to April 1st, or just current plan information? The awkwardness of the plan changes mid-year makes it hard."*

2. **[Freq: 2]** If a change is made mid-year, management sometimes wants it applied retroactively → current tools can't do this without a full rebuild
   > *"If there's a change mid-year but management wants it retroactive to January 1st — we have no clean way to do that."*

---

### Theme 2: Version Control Across Plan Periods
**Total frequency: 4 | 1 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 4]** No way to store and reference prior plan versions → when a mid-year change is made, the original plan is overwritten
   > *"Is there an area where those can be housed so that they can go back and reference them for mid-year and year-end?"*

## Finance × Leadership

**1 theme · 2 angles extracted**

### Theme 1: Accrual & Budget Impact of Mid-Year Changes
**Total frequency: 10 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 6]** Mid-year comp changes create downstream accrual re-estimates → Finance has to manually reforecast commission expense when plans change
   > *"There's so much manual labor having to update things when prices change, when revenue changes, and the commission amount changes and the dates change that go along with those commission payments."*

2. **[Freq: 4]** Plan changes mid-year affect commission expense recognition → Finance needs to see the financial impact before approving a change
   > *"A plan that made commercial sense at one stage can carry real margin exposure later — not because it was poorly designed, but because the business evolved around it and nobody modelled the impact of the mid-year adjustment."*

## Finance × Champion

**1 theme · 2 angles extracted**

### Theme 1: Reprocessing & Change Audit Trail
**Total frequency: 8 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 5]** When a mid-year change is made, Finance needs to reprocess historical periods → no audit trail of what was changed, when, and by whom
   > *"I mean, initially when we had the plan builder built out, we came across certain issues like mid-year plan changes — and the question is always: what happened before the change and what do we owe since?"*

2. **[Freq: 3]** Finance can't reconcile commission expense across periods when plan changes are applied retroactively → manual investigation required
   > *"Or let's say we have a plan change midway through the year where we change something from X to Y — the question is how do we go back and see what was running before, and make sure the numbers are right?"*

## Sales Comp × Leadership

**2 themes · 4 angles extracted**

### Theme 1: Mid-Year Quota Change & Rep Communication
**Total frequency: 17 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 10]** Quota changes mid-year create attainment confusion → if quota goes up, rep's attainment drops overnight and trust breaks
   > *"If you just update his quota mid-year from 100 to 300, then his attainment is going to go down — and he's going to want to understand why."*

2. **[Freq: 7]** Reps need to see updated plan documents when changes are made → no clean way to version and distribute updated plans currently
   > *"With mid-year quota changes, you can have it so they can look at their updated plan document — and if you want them to view the old one as well, that's totally fine."*

---

### Theme 2: Rep Promotion Mid-Year
**Total frequency: 12 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 8]** Reps getting promoted mid-year move between comp plans → current tools can't handle the plan transition cleanly without manual intervention
   > *"If Brian has to leave mid-year sometime, you can adjust that for Brian and say, okay, Brian will leave in 2026 because he's getting promoted — and the system needs to account for that transition cleanly."*

2. **[Freq: 4]** Promoted rep's prior attainment needs to carry forward correctly → today this is a manual calculation every time
   > *"In the global sales commission plan, he gets a promotion mid-year and he moves to a different team — and we need the history to follow him correctly."*

## Sales Comp × Champion

**2 themes · 4 angles extracted**

### Theme 1: New Hire Ramp & Quota Proration
**Total frequency: 74 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 42]** New hires joining mid-quarter need prorated quotas and ramped targets → manually calculated today, error-prone
   > *"Got a new person who has some kind of guarantee for a period of time and you've got a new person who may be ramping on a plan, and their quota may be successively increasing — it's a manual calc each time."*

2. **[Freq: 32]** Ramp percentages need to change as the rep progresses → today this requires manually updating quota records each month
   > *"If somebody is getting a mid-year promotion or somebody is on a contract for six months, you can preempt that and have a different plan duration — but right now we have to do that manually every time the ramp percentage changes."*

---

### Theme 2: Role & Territory Change Mid-Quarter
**Total frequency: 15 | 2 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 9]** Rep role changes mid-quarter require comp plan transition — current process means manually splitting attainment across old and new role
   > *"They're part of one plan for six months and then mid-year they are promoted to a different plan or to a different role — that's effectively stamped and you can track that over time."*

2. **[Freq: 6]** Territory reassignment mid-quarter creates crediting disputes → who gets credit for in-flight deals across the territory boundary
   > *"We were trying to manage to do like a territory change and it was an absolute headache."*
