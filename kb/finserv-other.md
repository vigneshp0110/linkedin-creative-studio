## Ops x Leadership

**8 themes · 20 angles extracted**

### Theme 1: Manual Commission Calculation Process
**Total frequency: 18 | 3 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 8]** Commission calculations done entirely in Excel with downloaded reports → error-prone, unsustainable, and time-consuming to maintain at scale
   > *"I inherited a very basic spreadsheet system from my boss and I've evolved over the years into something that's reasonably sophisticated but still very prone to errors, and it's rare for something to pass where something [doesn't go wrong]."*

2. **[Freq: 5]** Ops admins manually copying data across tabs, spreadsheets, and systems → mistakes slip through, catching them falls on one person
   > *"This is all manual process, right? So it's me making sure that when I copy these, you know, we've got on the bottom, it would have say 16 tabs right?"*

3. **[Freq: 5]** Commission calculations spread across multiple legacy or patchwork systems → fragile, hard to audit, and difficult to hand off
   > *"We went from an old legacy ERP system to a new ERP system, and commissions had been built... the document that I sent you, I know is partially accurate."*
