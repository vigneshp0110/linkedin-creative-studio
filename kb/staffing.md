**Total frequency: 18 | 5 angles**

**Angles** *(ranked by frequency)*:

1. **[Freq: 5]** Commission calculations run entirely on spreadsheets → one person carries all the operational burden and the process is fragile

   > *"Now, it's taking the data from, I would say not in the cleanest nicest looking spreadsheets or PDFS and making it into the actual data that we want to see."*

3. **[Freq: 4]** Commission operators must manually massage data from source systems to make plans work → time consumed in pre-processing rather than strategic work
   > *"My biggest thing is the data that I sent over in these excel spreadsheets, how easily is it for you guys to pull that and get the data, a gross margin number that we're looking for."*
